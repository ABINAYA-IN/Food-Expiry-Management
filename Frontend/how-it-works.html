Understand Product Expiry Status

Product status is calculated from the recorded expiry date.

FRESH

🟢 Fresh Products

Products with more than two days remaining before their expiry date.

EXPIRING SOON

🟠 Attention Needed

Products that expire today or within the next two days.

EXPIRED

🔴 Expired Products

Products whose expiry dates have passed. These should not be offered for purchase.

Designed for Customers and Sellers

Each role has its own features and responsibilities.

🛍️ For Customers

Discover products and review the available product information.

  • Browse available products.
  • Search for products by name.
  • Check prices and expiry dates.
  • Add eligible products to the cart.

🏪 For Sellers

Keep product information organized and monitor expiry dates.

  • Add new food products.
  • Update product prices and details.
  • Monitor expiry status.
  • Manage product inventory.
