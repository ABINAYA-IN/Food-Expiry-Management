About FreshTrack

FreshTrack is a smart food expiry management platform designed to help sellers manage food inventory and help customers discover available food products.

By organizing product details, prices, and expiry dates, FreshTrack aims to make food management easier, encourage responsible purchasing, and support the goal of reducing food waste.
